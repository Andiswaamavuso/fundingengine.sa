WHO WE ARE

Funding Engine is a South African SME funding and business growth company dedicated to helping entrepreneurs access the capital they need to start, grow, and scale their businesses. We believe that great business ideas deserve the opportunity to become successful businesses. However, we recognise that access to funding remains one of the biggest barriers to entrepreneurship and business growth in South Africa. Our mission is to bridge this gap by helping businesses build strong funding cases and achieve their funding success. Through strategic business assessments, identifying suitable funding opportunities, preparing and refining funding documentation, and managing the entire application process on your behalf, we take the complexity out of pursuing funding.

THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Every year, thousands of South African SMEs compete for limited funding from banks, government institutions, development finance institutions, and private investors. With funders receiving high volumes of applications and applying strict financial, compliance, and risk criteria, many promising businesses are rejected because they fail to demonstrate their financial strength, credibility, and funding readiness.

THE PERSON BEHIND FUNDING ENGINE

Meet the Founder

A commitment to helping businesses navigate funding opportunities and turn their growth ambitions into stronger funding applications.

Andiswa Shabalala

Financial Consultant

Andiswa Shabalala is the founder of Funding Engine, driven by a passion for entrepreneurship, financial empowerment and helping businesses access funding opportunities to achieve sustainable growth.

As an entrepreneur herself, having explored and pursued various business ventures, she understands the challenges of building a business and the importance of access to capital. With an LLB degree, a qualification in Financial Planning and professional experience in financial sales and business funding solutions, she brings together legal knowledge, financial insight and practical business experience. Through a practical, business-focused approach, she works with entrepreneurs to understand their funding needs, identify suitable funding solutions and strengthen their applications.

At Funding Engine, Andiswa is committed to bridging the gap between business potential and funding opportunities, empowering entrepreneurs to become funding-ready and driving better funding outcomes for businesses.
