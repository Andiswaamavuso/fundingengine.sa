WHAT WE DO

What's Included in the Service?

A structured process designed to present your business professionally and help you navigate the funding application journey.

01

Business & Funding Assessment

We review your business, funding requirements, objectives and readiness to establish what type of funding may be appropriate.

02

Funding Opportunity Identification

We identify potential funding institutions and solutions aligned with your business needs, industry and funding requirements.

03

Documentation Preparation

We assist with preparing, and refining business plans, financial information, funding proposals and supporting documents.

04

Application Completion

We help complete application forms and organise the supporting information required by the identified funding institution.

05

Application Submission

With your authorisation, we coordinate and facilitate submission of the application to the relevant funding institution.

06

Application Follow-up

We assist with application follow-ups and communicate requests for additional information or documentation where applicable.

FUNDING SOLUTIONS

The Right Funding Can Move Your Business Forward.

Every business has different funding needs. We help you identify suitable funding solutions, prepare a strong funding case and navigate the application process.

Grants Funding

Non-repayable funding designed to support business development, investment and economic growth.

What it covers

  • Business start-up and expansion
  • Machinery and equipment
  • Skills development and training
  • Innovation and technology
  • Job creation and transformation

Business Loans

Financing that provides businesses with capital to manage operations, pursue opportunities and grow.

What it covers

  • Working capital requirements
  • Business expansion
  • Stock and inventory purchases
  • Operational expenses
  • Growth and development projects

Asset Finance

Financing that enables businesses to acquire essential assets without paying the full cost upfront.

What it covers

  • Vehicles and delivery fleets
  • Manufacturing machinery
  • Production equipment
  • Technology and office equipment
  • Commercial and industrial assets

Invoice Discounting

Access cash tied up in unpaid customer invoices instead of waiting for payment terms to expire.

What it covers

  • Outstanding customer invoices
  • Short-term cash flow requirements
  • Supplier payments
  • Day-to-day operating costs
  • Bridging payment delays

Purchase Order Funding

Financing that helps businesses fulfil confirmed customer orders when they lack the upfront capital.

What it covers

  • Supplier and stock purchases
  • Raw materials and production
  • Manufacturing costs
  • Large customer orders
  • Order fulfilment and delivery

Equity Funding

Capital provided by investors in exchange for an ownership stake in a business, generally suited to businesses with strong growth potential.

What it covers

  • Business start-up capital
  • Scaling and expansion
  • Product development
  • Technology and innovation
  • Market expansion

Kruger Safari Adventures

Acquisition of additional safari vehicles

Operating in the tourism sector, this business offers guided game drive experiences to the world-renowned Kruger National Park. With growing ambitions to accommodate more visitors and expand its tour operations, the business required funding to acquire additional safari vehicles. The investment would increase passenger capacity, improve operational flexibility and enable the business to serve more local and international tourists.

Funding Secured R3,200,000
Drankensberg Chicken Farm

Farm expansion, breeding infrastructure and chick production facilities

This poultry farming business sought to expand its agricultural operations by introducing a dedicated breeding and chick production division. The proposed investment would support the development of additional farming infrastructure, specialised equipment and incubation facilities. This expansion would enable the business to strengthen its production capacity, establish a more integrated poultry operation and create additional agricultural value.

Funding Secured R500,000
