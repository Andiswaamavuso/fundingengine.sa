THE PROGRAMME

Build a business that is prepared for funding.

Access to funding is one of the biggest challenges facing startups and small businesses. But securing funding takes more than having a great business idea. Funders want to see businesses that are properly structured, financially disciplined, credit-conscious and capable of managing capital responsibly. The Funding Engine Funding Readiness Programme is a practical, six-month business development programme designed to help entrepreneurs build stronger, more credible and financially prepared businesses that are better positioned to access funding. Unlike traditional business training programmes that focus primarily on theory, our programme focuses on implementation. We work with entrepreneurs to strengthen their business foundations, improve financial management, develop responsible credit habits, build commercial credibility and prepare professional funding applications.

YOUR SIX-MONTH JOURNEY

The programme outline

Six focused stages that take your business from its current position towards stronger funding readiness.

01
MONTH ONE

Business Foundation

Objective: Establish the business's legal, operational and commercial foundations.

  • Complete a business fundability assessment
  • Verify CIPC registration, ownership and SARS compliance
  • Open a dedicated business bank account
  • Develop a professional business profile
  • Define the business model, target market and revenue streams
  • Develop a 6-month business development plan
02
MONTH TWO

Financial Management

Objective: Build the financial discipline and reporting systems required to demonstrate business performance.

  • Set up a bookkeeping and financial record-keeping system
  • Separate personal and business finances
  • Record and categorise all business income and expenses
  • Prepare a monthly income and expenditure report
  • Develop a business budget and 12-month cash-flow forecast
  • Organise all financial records, invoices and bank statements
03
MONTH THREE

Business Credit Building

Objective: Help the business establish a credible credit history and demonstrate responsible financial behaviour.

  • Review the business's credit profile and identify any adverse listings
  • Develop a credit improvement plan and address outstanding credit issues
  • Identify and apply for suitable supplier or trade accounts
  • Establish relationships with suppliers and request trade references where appropriate
  • Establish a consistent payment history by paying obligations on time
  • Implement a monthly credit monitoring and debt management routine
04
MONTH FOUR

Business Growth and Operations

Objective: Improve the business's ability to generate revenue, manage costs and demonstrate commercial viability.

  • Review product or service pricing and profitability
  • Develop a sales and marketing strategy
  • Establish monthly sales and revenue targets
  • Build a customer database and sales pipeline
  • Identify strategic suppliers, partners and growth opportunities
  • Implement operational procedures and monthly performance tracking
05
MONTH FIVE

Funding Preparation

Objective: Prepare the business to approach funders with a credible, complete and well-supported funding proposal.

  • Conduct a second fundability assessment and review progress
  • Develop or update the business plan
  • Prepare 3-year financial projections and cash-flow forecasts
  • Determine the funding requirement and prepare a funding utilisation plan
  • Compile all supporting documents, including financial, legal and business records
  • Identify suitable funders and develop a targeted funding strategy
06
MONTH SIX

Funding Applications and Execution

Objective: Move from preparation to targeted funding applications while establishing systems to manage the business after funding.

  • Complete the final fundability assessment
  • Finalise the business funding proposal and application documents
  • Match the business with suitable funding opportunities
  • Prepare and submit funding applications to relevant institutions
  • Track applications, follow up with funders and respond to additional requirements
  • Develop a 12-month post-programme business growth and financial management plan

The Expected Outcomes

By the end of the six-month programme, participants should have a properly structured, financially disciplined and funding-ready business, supported by sound financial management practices, an improved credit profile or actionable credit-building plan, a sustainable business model, and a complete funding application portfolio that positions them to pursue suitable funding opportunities and achieve long-term business growth.

Outcomes depend on each participant's starting point, engagement, and implementation.

✓

Stronger Business Foundations

A properly structured business with improved registration, governance, compliance and business documentation.

✓

Improved Financial Management

✓

Stronger Credit Awareness and Creditworthiness

A clear understanding of personal and business credit profiles, an actionable credit improvement plan and responsible credit management habits.

✓

Improved Business Sustainability and Growth

A more developed business model, defined revenue streams, improved sales strategies and stronger operational management.

✓

Increased Funding Readiness
